Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email and password (hashed — we can never read it back). When you place an order we keep your shipping address and order details. If you use the newsletter we store your email address for sending updates.

2. How we use it

We use your data to fulfil orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and — only with your subscription — send you product news. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and to remember the products in your shopping cart. No tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Your rights

You can view or edit your profile anytime, unsubscribe from the newsletter in one click, and email us to request deletion of your account.

5. Contact

Privacy questions? Write to support@shopsphere.example.
